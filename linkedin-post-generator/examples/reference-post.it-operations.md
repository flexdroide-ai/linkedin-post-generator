This is a reference example for tone, structure and editorial logic.

---

## SOURCE NEWS / SIGNAL
A Gartner report states that only a limited share of AI use cases in Infrastructure & Operations fully meets ROI expectations, while a non-trivial portion fails.

The exact value is not the point of the example.
The editorial logic is.

---

## WHAT THE POST SHOULD NOT DO
Do NOT just say:
“Gartner says AI projects fail, therefore companies should be careful.”

That is too generic and useless.

---

## THE RIGHT EDITORIAL ANGLE
The correct angle is something like:

- the real issue is no longer whether companies are “adopting AI”
- the real issue is whether AI is being embedded into real operational workflows
- value depends on:
  - process integration
  - data quality
  - governance
  - adoption by teams
  - measurable KPIs
  - realistic use cases

Good examples of concrete use cases to mention:
- knowledge management support
- ticket classification / triage
- incident support
- service desk augmentation
- cloud operations support
- decision support in I&O

---

## EXAMPLE OF POST DIRECTION

Possible opening logic:
> Il problema non è più adottare l’AI. È farla funzionare davvero.

Then:
- mention Gartner and the core data point
- explain why this matters for ITSM / I&O / service delivery
- say that many AI initiatives fail when they are treated as a showcase rather than an operational capability
- explain that AI does not replace operational maturity; it amplifies it
- close with a concrete question to the network

---

## WHAT MAKES THIS A GOOD POST
It works because it:
- uses a real news item as a hook
- moves quickly from the news to the managerial implication
- avoids generic “AI is the future” rhetoric
- positions the author as someone who thinks in terms of workflows, delivery, service quality and operational value
